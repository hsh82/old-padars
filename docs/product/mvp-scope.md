# MVP scope

## In scope

- Shared-site school tenancy and school membership.
- Education academic records for students, programs, courses, annual program enrollment, student groups, instructors, and assessments.
- Annual School Course Offering records.
- Persistent Moodle/MuTMS mappings, enrollment state, and sync operations.
- A mock-backed course provisioning vertical slice with retryable operation state.

## Deferred

Finance, payroll, messaging, advanced timetabling, gamification, full report-card publication, live SSO, and live MuTMS provisioning remain outside the first slice.
